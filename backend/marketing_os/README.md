# NMS Marketing OS

Marketing OS is an isolated marketing intelligence and execution layer.

It does not own:
- patient clinical records
- telehealth
- secure messages
- authentication
- billing
- the public website
- the existing Content Strategist publishing queue

Initial responsibilities:
- marketing goals
- budgets
- attribution
- performance analytics
- AI recommendations
- approval/policy controls
- SEO intelligence
- backlink intelligence
- competitor intelligence

External publishing and advertising writes remain disabled until explicitly enabled.
